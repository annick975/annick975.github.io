Greatest Matches

UCL Final 2012

Chelsea 1(4) - 1(3) Bayern Munich

The night Didier Drogba cemented his legacy, scoring the equalizer and winning penalty as Chelsea won their first Champions League title in a dramatic shootout at Bayern's home stadium.

UCL Final 2021

Manchester City 0 - 1 Chelsea

Kai Havertz's first-half goal secured Chelsea's second Champions League triumph under Thomas Tuchel, with a tactically perfect performance against Pep Guardiola's Manchester City.

UCL Semi-Final 2009

Chelsea 4 - 4 Liverpool

One of the most thrilling European nights at Stamford Bridge as Chelsea edged past Liverpool in an eight-goal thriller to reach the Champions League semi-finals.

Premier League Clincher 2010

Chelsea 8 - 0 Wigan Athletic

Carlo Ancelotti's side secured the Premier League title in style, thrashing Wigan to complete the Double and setting a Premier League record with 103 goals scored in a season.
