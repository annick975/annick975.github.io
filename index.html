My Dream Destinations

Five places I hope to visit someday

Santorini, Greece

Famous for its stunning sunsets, white-washed buildings with blue domes, and crystal-clear waters.

Kyoto, Japan

A city of ancient temples, traditional gardens, and geisha culture.

Machu Picchu, Peru

This ancient Incan citadel set high in the Andes Mountains offers mysterious ruins and breathtaking vistas.

Bora Bora, French Polynesia

A tropical paradise with overwater bungalows, turquoise lagoons, and coral reefs.

Serengeti, Tanzania

Witness the spectacular wildlife and vast savanna landscapes of Africa.
